# 🗺️ ScaleTools Strategic Roadmap (v1.0)

This roadmap synthesizes the "Project Origin" vision (Third-Party Profits) into a modular, engineering-grade execution plan for **ScaleTools**.

---

## 🏗️ Phase 1–6: The Foundation (COMPLETED)
*Status: Established & Functional*

- [x] **Supplier Truth Layer**: Deterministic identity system and CRM-style status flow.
- [x] **Profit Intelligence**: Heuristic matching and risk-adjusted margin calculation.
- [x] **Market Signal Engine**: Real-world validation of demand, trends, and stability.
- [x] **Temporal Memory**: Historical snapshot storage with confidence decay logic.
- [x] **Operator Cockpit**: Structured sidebar navigation and Ctrl+K Command Palette.

---

## 🚀 Phase 7: The Procurement Engine (Next Move)
*Objective: Transform intelligence into action.*

### 🛠️ Key Features:
1. **Purchase Order (PO) Builder**: 
   - Move products from "Opportunities" into "Draft POs."
   - Live Amazon data refresh during PO creation.
   - Export-ready formats for suppliers (CSV/PDF).
2. **Supplier Opportunity Ranking**: 
   - Roll up product scores to the supplier level.
   - "Which supplier should I call today to spend $10k most profitably?"
3. **Master Product Folder**: 
   - A centralized, hard-saved database of every vetted ASIN.
   - Tracking products even when currently unprofitable (waiting for price shifts).

---

## 🕵️ Phase 8: Storefront & Brand Intelligence
*Objective: Reverse-engineering the competition.*

### 🛠️ Key Features:
1. **Storefront Stalker**: 
   - Ingest competitor storefront URLs.
   - Cross-reference their catalog against your existing supplier network.
   - Identify "hidden" suppliers by matching brand overlaps.
2. **Brand Research Module**: 
   - Pull every ASIN for a target brand.
   - Match brand catalogs against your internal distributor list.
3. **Multi-Pack Discovery**: 
   - Intelligence to identify high-margin bundle/multipack opportunities.

---

## 📈 Phase 9: Inventory & Growth Analytics
*Objective: Managing the cash cycle.*

### 🛠️ Key Features:
1. **Smart Restocker**: 
   - Integration of sales velocity to predict OOS (Out of Stock).
   - Automated reorder queue.
2. **Inventory Profit Tracker**: 
   - Real-time visibility into "Cash on Hand" vs. "Cash in Inbound."
   - Tracking unfulfillable vs. receiving inventory status.
3. **VA/Buyer Accountability**: 
   - Tagging every sourcing lead to a specific user.
   - Profit-per-employee analytics.

---

## 🤖 Phase 10: Autonomous Decision Engine
*Objective: High-leverage automation.*

### 🛠️ Key Features:
1. **Autonomous Sourcing Agent**: 
   - Continuous background scraping and matching.
   - Slack/Email alerts for "Golden Opportunities" (High Score + High Confidence).
2. **Margin Decay Protection**: 
   - Automated alerts when active product margins drop below thresholds.
3. **Wholesale Pricing Leverage**: 
   - Historical spend tracking to negotiate better tiers with distributors.

---

## 🧭 Design Principles (The "ScaleTools Way")
1. **No Spreadsheet Prison**: If a task requires a spreadsheet, we failed.
2. **Explainable AI**: No black boxes; every score must be traceable to market signals.
3. **Truth over Simulation**: Real Keepa/Amazon data always overrides heuristics.
4. **Operator Cockpit**: High-density information, minimal noise.

---

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ScaleTools is not just an app; it is a **Decision Operating System** for wholesale arbitrage. Every feature added must reduce the time between "Data In" and "Profit Realized."
